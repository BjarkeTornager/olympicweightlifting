# Inter 4.1

Unmodified `web/InterVariable.woff2` from the official [Inter 4.1 release](https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1). Copyright The Inter Project Authors. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `LICENSE.txt`.

Loaded with `next/font/local`, served by this application and included in its offline asset cache. No third-party font request is needed at runtime or during builds.

Inter was chosen for its screen-oriented text design and tabular numerals. Typography uses regular body text, restrained semibold headings, distinguishable I/l variants and a minimum 12 px for compact labels. See [the typeface documentation](https://rsms.me/inter/) for its design and OpenType features.
